The freelancer CRM that fits how you work

You don't need a huge CRM to manage your clients. Kanlane gives you just enough: clients, contacts, tasks and meetings in one place, without endless configuration.

Why a lightweight CRM instead of a full one?

Traditional CRMs are built for sales teams with funnels, campaigns and lots of setup. A freelancer needs something else: knowing which client asks for what, when it's due and who to talk to. Kanlane covers that without asking you to build a system.

Clients and contacts

A card per client with their people, phones and notes, without fields you'll never use.

Tasks per client

What you have on hand with each client, on their own board and with deadlines.

Meetings and dates

Deliveries and meetings in a calendar that makes sense at a glance.

Built for a freelancer's day to day

Kanlane runs in the browser and can be installed as an app; it keeps your latest data for offline work. Your clients' passwords are encrypted in your browser with a master password. And you can export your data whenever you want, because it's yours.

One client, all their context at hand

When you work with several clients at once, what matters is recovering the context in seconds: what to do, who to talk to and when. Here's how Kanlane brings it together.

How to start in three steps

  1. Create your account. With email, Google or GitHub, or join as a guest just to try.
  2. Choose the project type. Support, development, kanban or one made to measure.
  3. Add your clients and tasks. Start with whatever you have on hand; the order follows.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a full CRM?

If you only manage clients and projects, probably not. Kanlane covers clients, contacts, tasks and meetings without the complexity of a sales CRM.

Is Kanlane free?

You can create a free account and no card is required to start. Always check the current conditions inside the service.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export clients, contacts, tasks, meetings and encrypted passwords whenever you want.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The public demo lets you try a board with sample data, and guest access keeps data in your browser.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can install Kanlane as an app and open it offline: you'll see your latest data and changes will sync when you're back online.

Can I manage clients and projects at once?

Yes. Each project is an independent board with its own data, and clients connect to their tasks, contacts and meetings.

Try the board before deciding

Drag a card, open a task and see how it connects to its client. It's a demo with sample data; no account needed.

If the board doesn't load, open the demo in a separate page.
