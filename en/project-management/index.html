How to organize a project step by step

  1. Define the outcome. Write what must be done and by when. A concrete delivery is easier to plan than a generic to-do list.
  2. Split the work. Create small, assignable tasks, with subtasks when a step has several parts.
  3. Design the stages. A simple board can start with "Pending", "In progress" and "Done". Add columns only if your flow needs them.
  4. Give the team visibility. Use deadlines and assignees so you know who works on each task and what needs attention.
  5. Review the context. Connect meetings and contacts with the client and adjust the board after each delivery.

This flow works for development, design, support, marketing and other work that moves through stages. In Kanlane you can start from a template or define your own project.

An example: a website for a client

Split the project into content, design, development and review phases. Add the kickoff meeting to the calendar, create a task to receive the copy and another to review the homepage. Assign each task to a person and note the deadline. When a client question arrives, you'll have the contact, the next meeting and the board status at hand.

What to check before choosing a manager

Try how tasks are created and moved, whether you can see dates on a calendar, how projects are shared and what happens offline. If you manage clients, also check where contacts are stored and how to export your data. The Kanlane demo lets you try the board without signing up.

Frequently asked questions

What project types can I create?

Support, development, kanban or one made to measure, with or without clients. Without a predefined type, you can define your own stages and colors.

Can I work in a team?

Yes. You can share a project, invite people by email and assign tasks. Each person has a role: owner, editor or viewer.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can install Kanlane as an app and open it offline: you'll see your latest data and changes will sync when you're back online.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export tasks, notes, clients, contacts, meetings and encrypted passwords whenever you want.

Is Kanlane free?

You can create a free account and no card is required to start. Always check the current conditions inside the service.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The public demo lets you try a board with sample data, and guest access keeps data in your browser.
