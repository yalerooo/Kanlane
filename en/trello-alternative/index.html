Why choose Kanlane as a Trello alternative?

Trello organizes work with boards, lists and cards. Kanlane also uses kanban boards, but puts the project and the client at the center: you can check their tasks, contacts, meetings and credentials from a single app. If you juggle several jobs at once, that context helps you know what's missing and who to talk to.

Boards and tasks

Organize work by columns, assign people, add deadlines, subtasks, labels and notes.

Calendar and meetings

Review deliveries and meetings by day, week or month to understand what comes next.

Clients and team

Connect contacts and projects, share boards and set owner, editor or viewer permissions.

Before switching from Trello

Kanlane doesn't offer direct import of Trello boards yet. If you have many boards or rely on Trello automations and integrations, check first whether the switch fits. Trello has its own automations and Power-Ups and integrations; Kanlane prioritizes a simple space for projects and clients.

  1. Try a project. Open the demo or create a board with a real job.
  2. Reproduce your flow. Define columns, add a few tasks and check the calendar.
  3. Invite your team. Review what each role can do before moving more work.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kanlane free?

You can create a free account and no card is required to start. Always check the current conditions inside the service.

Can I try Kanlane without signing up?

Yes. The public demo lets you try a board with sample data, and guest access keeps data in your browser.

Can I import my Trello boards?

Direct import isn't available in this version. You can start with a new project and export your Kanlane data whenever you want.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can install Kanlane as an app and open it offline: you'll see your latest data and changes will sync when you're back online.

Can I work in a team?

Yes. You can share a project, invite people by email and assign tasks. Each person has a role: owner, editor or viewer.

What happens with my passwords?

They are encrypted in your browser with your master password before being saved, and that password never leaves your device. Your vault is personal and isn't shared with the team automatically.

Try a board before deciding

Drag a card, open a task and try its subtasks. It's a demo with sample data; no account needed.

If the board doesn't load, open the demo in a separate page.
