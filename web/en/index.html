01 · Board

A board for every project

Drag tasks between stages. Choose a support, development or kanban board, or define the stages you need, with per-column limits.

  • Quick filters by client and by what is due this week
  • Board or list view
  • Stages, colors and limits made to measure

02 · Tasks

Subtasks and recurrences

Split a task into steps and make it repeat every week, month or year. The next one appears on its own when you finish.

  • Progress visible on every card
  • Notes with images and links to contacts and passwords
  • Due dates that alert you when it is time

03 · Calendar

The week, at a glance

Meetings and deadlines from all your clients in one calendar. Switch between month, week and day, and filter by client when you want to focus.

  • Drag to change the date
  • Video call links with a "Join" button
  • Reminders of what's due and what's about to start

04 · Passwords

Your clients' keys, safe

Store email, server, remote desktop and other access, next to the client it belongs to. Sensitive data is encrypted in your browser and only decrypted when you ask.

  • Encrypted in the browser with your master password
  • A recovery key you can download and keep
  • Link them to a task to keep them at hand

Security and privacy

Built to hold other people's data

You work with your clients' information. That's why Kanlane tries to know the minimum and protect the maximum.

Encrypted in your browser

Passwords and their notes are encrypted with AES-256 before leaving your device: without your master password or recovery key, no one can read them. When you create a project you can choose to encrypt the rest of its content too.

Your data, only for you

Each account only accesses its own data and the projects shared with it. You can export a copy of your data whenever you want.

No ads or third-party analytics

No ads or third-party analytics, and fonts are served from the site itself. More details in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask

How much does Kanlane cost?

Creating an account is free and no card is required. If paid plans ever appear, they will be announced first and what you already have stays yours.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can install Kanlane as an app on your phone or computer and open it offline: you will see your latest data and changes will sync when you are back online.

Who can see my passwords?

Only you. Passwords and their notes are encrypted in your browser with your master password before being saved, and that password never leaves your device.

What if I forget my master password?

When you create it, a recovery key is generated that you can download and keep. Without the master password or that key, no one can decrypt your passwords, not even Kanlane.

Can a whole project be encrypted?

Yes, you choose it when you create each project. With “Full encryption”, the content (tasks, notes, clients, contacts, meetings, passwords, images and plugin data) is encrypted in your browser with AES-256 and a password only you know: Kanlane doesn't have it and can't read that content, and if you lose the password and the recovery key the project can't be recovered. With “Managed by Kanlane” there is no extra password: it protects you if someone copies the database, but Kanlane holds the key and could technically decrypt the project. In both cases the project name, columns, labels, dates and task status are not encrypted, and those projects can't be synced with GitHub.

Can I work in a team?

Yes. You can share a project, invite people by email and assign tasks. Each person has a role: owner, editor or viewer.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. You can export tasks, notes, clients, contacts, meetings and encrypted passwords. There are also local versions and optional encrypted copies in your account. The export doesn't include original images or plugin data; keep separately what you need.

What are plugins?

Small extensions that add features, like per-project hour tracking or a timer. They run isolated and only access what you allow them to.

Can I connect Claude or another AI assistant?

Yes. Kanlane has an MCP server: you create a token in Settings → Integrations and your assistant can read the tasks of that project, move them between columns, add notes to them and create new tasks. The token is valid for a single project, it can be read-only and you can revoke it whenever you want. It does not work with encrypted projects, and Kanlane does not include an assistant of its own.
